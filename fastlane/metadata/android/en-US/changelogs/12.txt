Sync: keep your vault in sync across your phones, end-to-end encrypted, through your own WebDAV server (Nextcloud, ownCloud or another server). Settings > Across phones > Sync. Set up a new phone from sync, or join a phone with its own vault to the synced one.

Backups made by this version cannot be opened by 1.7.0 or earlier; the older app reports a newer backup. The terms of use were updated and are asked once more.
